  • Questions are drawn at random and mixed to match the exam blueprint. No question repeats within a sitting.
  • You can change any answer, move freely with Previous and Next, and flag anything you want to come back to.
  • No right or wrong is shown, and no explanations appear, until you submit.
  • A review screen at the end lists every question with its status so you can revisit before submitting.
  • When the clock reaches zero the exam submits itself with whatever you have answered.
  • You can pause the clock whenever you need to. The question is hidden while paused, and paused time is excluded from the pacing figures in your report.
  • Leaving without pausing does not stop the clock, exactly as it would not in a proctored sitting.
